# Legal, Regulatory and Corporate History

**Document ID:** KD-LEG-013 · **Version:** 1.4 · **Owner:** Aaron Feldstein, General Counsel
**Approved:** 4 February 2026 · **Next review:** 4 February 2027
**Maps to:** SOC 2 CC2.3, CC3.4 · ISO 27001:2022 A.5.31, A.5.34, A.5.36

## 1. Litigation

**Kestrel Data is not a party to any pending litigation relating to data protection,
privacy, or information security**, and has not been since incorporation in 2019.

The company has been party to two commercial disputes in total, neither involving personal
data, security, or availability:

| Matter | Year | Nature | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Data, Inc. v. Northbeam Analytics LLC | 2022 | Trademark opposition | Settled by coexistence agreement, 11 November 2022 |
| Vendor fee dispute (confidential, arbitration) | 2024 | Contractual, professional services vendor | Settled 3 April 2024, no admission, no ongoing obligation |

## 2. Regulatory enforcement

**Kestrel Data has never been subject to a regulatory enforcement action, fine, consent
decree, undertaking, or formal investigation** by any data protection authority, sectoral
regulator, or competition authority. No supervisory authority has issued Kestrel a
reprimand, order, or corrective measure under Article 58 of the GDPR.

Kestrel has never made a personal data breach notification to a supervisory authority
under Article 33, because no personal data breach meeting the notification threshold has
occurred. Incident history is in KD-SEC-008 §9.

## 3. Complaints

Two data subject complaints have been received directly since 2019, both from individuals
whose employers were Kestrel customers, and both resolved by directing the complainant to
their employer as controller with an explanation of the roles. Neither complainant escalated
to a supervisory authority.

## 4. Corporate structure and change of control

Kestrel Data, Inc. is a Delaware C-corporation incorporated on 8 April 2019, privately
held, with a single class of common stock and two preferred series (Series A, 2021;
Series B, 2023). There are no subsidiaries other than **Kestrel Data Ireland Limited**
(company number 712884, registered in Dublin), which employs EU personnel and is the
Article 27 EU establishment.

**There has been no change of control, merger, acquisition, or material corporate
restructuring**, and none is pending. Should one occur, customers would be notified under
the assignment clause of the master agreement, which requires notice and — for a
competitor acquirer — grants the customer a termination right.

## 5. Insolvency and going concern

Kestrel has never entered any insolvency, administration, receivership, or similar
proceeding, and no such proceeding is threatened. The most recent audited financial
statements were issued without a going-concern qualification.

## 6. Legal hold

Kestrel operates a documented legal hold process, owned by the General Counsel. On
becoming aware of reasonably anticipated litigation or an investigation, the General
Counsel issues a written hold notice identifying custodians and data sources, and
suspension of the ordinary retention schedule is applied in Google Vault and by disabling
the relevant retention jobs. Holds are reviewed quarterly and released in writing.

Two holds have been issued since 2019, both in connection with the matters in §1, both
released — the last on 19 May 2024. **No legal hold is currently in force.** A hold
overrides the retention schedule (KD-LEG-002 §5) and, where it conflicts with a deletion
instruction from a customer, Kestrel tells the customer that a hold applies and why.

## 7. Regulatory scope

Kestrel is not a regulated financial institution, is not a covered entity or business
associate under HIPAA (see KD-LEG-014 §4), is not a federal contractor, and is not subject
to sectoral supervision. Its obligations arise from general data protection law, its
contracts, and the certifications it chooses to maintain.

## 8. Statements in this document

Every statement above is confirmed by the General Counsel as at the approval date and is
restated at each annual review. Where a customer requires a contemporaneous confirmation,
the General Counsel will provide one in writing at the point of contracting; several
customers require this annually and it is provided as a matter of course.
