# Master Subscription Agreement — Summary of Key Terms

**Document ID:** KD-LEG-014 · **Version:** 3.2 · **Owner:** Aaron Feldstein, General Counsel
**Approved:** 28 January 2026 · **Next review:** 28 January 2027
**Maps to:** SOC 2 CC2.3, CC9.1 · GDPR Article 28

*A summary for review purposes. The executed agreement governs; where this summary and an
executed contract differ, the contract prevails.*

## 1. Structure

A Kestrel engagement comprises the Master Subscription Agreement, an Order Form, the Data
Processing Agreement (KD-LEG-001) with its Annex 2 technical measures (KD-LEG-003), and
the Security Addendum (KD-LEG-005). The DPA and Security Addendum are incorporated by
reference into every agreement; they are not optional extras negotiated case by case.

## 2. Governing law and venue

The standard agreement is governed by the **laws of the State of Delaware**, excluding its
conflict of laws rules, with exclusive venue in the state and federal courts of
Wilmington, Delaware. For customers contracting with Kestrel Data Ireland Limited, the
standard governing law is **Ireland**, with venue in Dublin. The UN Convention on Contracts
for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

Governing law is negotiable for enterprise agreements; venue in a neutral jurisdiction and
arbitration under ICC rules have both been agreed in past contracts.

## 3. Liability

The standard limitation of liability is **12 months' fees paid** in the preceding twelve
months. A **super-cap of 3× fees** applies to breaches of confidentiality, breaches of the
data protection obligations, and Kestrel's indemnities. Liability for gross negligence,
wilful misconduct, death or personal injury, and each party's indemnification obligations
is uncapped to the extent the law does not permit limitation.

Kestrel indemnifies the customer for third-party intellectual property infringement claims
arising from the service.

## 4. HIPAA and Business Associate Agreements

**Kestrel does not sign Business Associate Agreements and does not accept protected health
information.** Kestrel Insight is not designed for PHI, is not HITECH-configured, and the
Acceptable Use terms prohibit uploading it. **Zero BAAs are currently executed.** A customer
whose use case requires PHI should not select Kestrel for that workload, and Kestrel will
say so during procurement rather than after signature.

## 5. Confidentiality

Mutual confidentiality obligations apply for the term plus **five years**, and
**indefinitely for personal data and for trade secrets** for as long as they remain trade
secrets. Personnel obligations are covered in KD-SEC-013 §2.

## 6. Audit rights

Customers may audit Kestrel's compliance with the DPA **once per twelve-month period on 30
days' written notice**, with additional audits permitted following a personal data breach
affecting the customer or on a supervisory authority's instruction. Audits are conducted
during business hours, must not unreasonably disrupt operations, and are subject to
confidentiality.

Kestrel first offers the SOC 2 Type II report, the ISO 27001 certificate, the penetration
test executive summary, and a completed CAIQ, and in practice these satisfy the majority of
audit requests. **Where a customer's regulator or internal policy requires an on-site or
questionnaire-based audit, Kestrel will accommodate it** — the report-first position is a
starting point, not a refusal. Two customer audits were conducted during 2025, both
document-based, both closed without findings.

## 7. Term, termination and transition

The standard term is 12 months with automatic renewal on 30 days' notice of non-renewal.
Either party may terminate for material breach uncured after 30 days, or immediately on
the other's insolvency.

On termination the customer retains access to export their data for **30 days**, after
which live data is deleted within 30 days and backups expire on the ordinary rotation
within a further 35 days (KD-LEG-002 §1). A certificate of deletion is provided on request.

## 8. Assignment and change of control

Neither party may assign without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or
sale of substantially all assets, subject to notice. Where the acquirer is a competitor of
the customer, the customer may terminate within 30 days of notice.

## 9. Subcontracting

Sub-processing is governed by the DPA: a public list (KD-LEG-006), **30 days' notice**
before adding or replacing a sub-processor, a right to object on reasonable data protection
grounds, and termination without penalty if the objection cannot be resolved.

## 10. Service levels

The availability commitment, service credits, and support response targets are in the
Availability SLA (KD-ENG-001). Service credits are the sole remedy for missed availability,
with a termination right if the monthly commitment is missed in three consecutive months.
